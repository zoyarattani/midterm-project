ABOUT THE NOOK

Why I Made This

I love finding a book that is so good I immediately want to tell someone else to read it. Zoya's Book Nook is my place to collect a few of those favorites.

My bookshelf is usually a mix of romance, fantasy, and thrillers, so I wanted this site to feel like browsing through my own little collection instead of just looking at a list of titles.

For readers who always say, "just one more chapter."
